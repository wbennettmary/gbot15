Merge Engine Architecture

TemplateMergeEngine extracts known editable regions, generates original replacement content, validates replacements, inserts into known regions, rebuilds HTML, then verifies protected structure was not changed. The complete static HTML is not sent to AI for unrestricted rewriting.
